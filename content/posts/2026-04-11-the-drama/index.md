The reveal at the heart of *The Drama* is so central to how the whole thing unfolds that writing around it feels like describing a magic trick by only talking about the magician's sleeves. Instead of spoiling the magic show, I’ll instead, tell you what it felt like.

It's refreshingly funny. Funnier than I expected even, in a way I haven't seen cinema be in awhile. Zendaya's and Pattinson's performances clearly demonstrate why only they could be cast as the leads for this and Alana Haim needs to be considered in more roles where she gets to flex her sass. 

The humour is witty and intelligent, with an editing style that carries the slapstick instinct without anyone needing to slip on anything. The comedic fallout of the central secret is threaded through the film with enough restraint that it never overstays its welcome. 

What surprised me most was how the film holds its comedic tone across what is a genuinely heavy subject matter. There were a couple of confusing moments for me where I wasn't entirely sure if the film wanted me to laugh or deliberate, but those were brief and didn't leave a mark on my experience of the film. The balance is handled with confidence overall.

This is unmistakably A24 in its look, its texture, its framing of discomfort as something to sit inside rather than resolve. But what's more interesting is that it actually watches like early A24, back when the label felt like a signal rather than a brand. That particular kind of craft, restrained and precise and confident in its own strangeness, has been harder to find from them lately. It's good to be reminded they can still do it.

*The Drama* is the best film I've seen so far this year and I suggest going in knowing as little as possible for the best experience of sipping tea you'll have in the cinema. 

## Verdict

**4.5 / 5** — Sharp, warm, and funnier than it has any right to be given what it's actually about.

**Best for:** Anyone who goes in fresh and has people to debrief with after.

**Skip if:** You're determined not to have a good night.
