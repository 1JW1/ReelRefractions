There's a version of this review that doesn't exist, and it's the useful one — the one where I can actually tell you what happens. The reveal at the heart of *The Drama* is so central to how the whole thing unfolds that writing around it feels like describing a magic trick by only talking about the magician's sleeves. So instead, I'll tell you what it felt like.

It felt funny. More funny than I expected, and I expected some.

The humour is witty in the way that earns it. Not telegraphed, not loud, but intelligent, with an editing style that carries the slapstick instinct without anyone needing to slip on anything. The comedic fallout of the central secret is threaded through the film with enough restraint that it never overstays its welcome. You keep laughing, and the film keeps earning it. The whole screening was laughing. That's not nothing.

What surprised me most was how the film holds its tone across what is, when you step back, genuinely heavy subject matter. There were a couple of moments where I wasn't entirely sure if the film wanted me to laugh or sit with something, but those moments were brief and didn't leave a mark. The balance is handled with more confidence than most films manage.

This is unmistakably A24 in its look, its texture, its framing of discomfort as something to sit inside rather than resolve. But what's more interesting is that it actually watches like early A24, back when the label felt like a signal rather than a brand. That particular kind of craft, restrained and precise and confident in its own strangeness, has been harder to find from them lately. It's good to be reminded they can still do it.

I'll say this plainly: this is the best film I've seen this year, and we're far enough in that I mean it with some conviction. Go in knowing as little as possible. If you've been following the press tour closely, some of the magic may already be gone. But if you haven't, guard that ignorance.

Solo works. But bring people if you can. This is the kind of film you'll end up referencing amongst yourselves for a while.

## Verdict

**4 / 5** — Sharp, warm, and funnier than it has any right to be given what it's actually about.

**Best for:** Anyone who goes in fresh and has people to debrief with after.

**Skip if:** You've already had the central secret spoiled — manage your expectations accordingly.
